# Case Study: Education Data Analytics Platform

## Overview
Role: Senior Developer / Technical Lead | Duration: 2017-2019 at OneTeam US
Platform: Michigan government & education data analytics, 800+ school districts

## Challenge
Build a comprehensive education data analytics platform aggregating multiple public data sources for government transparency, fiscal benchmarking, and school performance tracking.

## What Was Built
### ETL Pipelines
- Aggregated large-scale public datasets: NCES (National Center for Education Statistics), M-STEP (Michigan state assessments), NWEA (MAP Growth test scores), district financials, census data
- Data aggregated into normalized schemas for cross-source analysis
- Automated refresh cycles for updated public datasets

### Analytics & Dashboards
- Tableau integration for advanced reporting and analytics
- Custom Angular/D3.js dashboards with role-based access levels
- Custom reports for district comparisons, fiscal benchmarking
- School performance tracking across Michigan

## Technologies
Node.js, Express, Angular 5, D3.js, Tableau, MySQL, ETL Pipelines, NCES, M-STEP, NWEA MAP Growth, Census Data
